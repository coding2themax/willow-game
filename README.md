# Lone Cowboy

A small browser game after *Lone Cowboy: My Life* by Will James (1930), which entered the US public domain on January 1, 2026.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no dependencies (fonts load from Google Fonts, with serif fallbacks).

## Chapters

| | Chapter | How to play |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bopy's Trapline**: run the winter trapline on snowshoes | Space / Up / tap to jump, hold for a higher leap. Grab pelts, reach the cabin. |
| 2 | **Drifting South**: ride alone toward the cow country | Same controls. Clear sagebrush, fences, badger holes and tumbleweeds; grab canteens before your water runs out. |
| 3 | **The Rough String**: ride three broncs to the whistle | Left/Right or A/D, or hold the left/right half of the screen. Keep the needle in the green for 8 seconds. |
| 4 | **Spring Roundup**: haze strays into the corral | Arrow keys / WASD, or touch and hold where you want to ride. Pen 8 of 12 head before sundown. |

Then an epilogue with your tally and a rank, from Greenhorn to Lone Cowboy.

Progress is saved at the start of each chapter. `M` or the speaker button toggles sound.

The story cards paraphrase the book's account of James's life; all art is drawn in code.
